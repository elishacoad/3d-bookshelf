# Bookshelf — references

Inspiration and prior art for the two directions (`src/shelves/css`,
`src/shelves/gl`).

## Primary

- **Stripe Press** — <https://press.stripe.com/>
  Volumes suspended in a dark void, lying flat and read nearly edge-on, scrolled
  through rather than browsed across. The spine is a horizontal band you read
  left-to-right; the cover lies on top of it, foreshortened almost to nothing.
  Was built as a third direction and later removed in favour of a horizontal
  shelf.

- **grizz.fyi** — <https://grizz.fyi/>
  The closest thing to the target. Books are spines at rest; hovering pulls one
  out of the row and turns it to show its cover. Flat elevation with no
  perspective shear at rest, which is what the `projection: orthographic`
  option exists to reproduce.
